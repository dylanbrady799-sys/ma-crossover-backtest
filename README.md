# Moving Average Crossover Backtest

A Python backtest of a 20/50-day simple moving average (SMA) crossover strategy on SPY, compared with buy-and-hold from March 2015 to November 2026.

## Strategy

- **Buy** when the 20-day SMA crosses above the 50-day SMA.
- **Sell** (move to cash) when the 20-day SMA crosses below the 50-day SMA.
- Signals are observed at the close and traded the next day, so the backtest does not use information that was not yet available (no look-ahead bias).
- Each entry and exit costs 5 basis points. Cash earns 0%.

## Results

SPY, 2015-03-16 to 2026-11-13, 5 bps per trade:

| Metric | MA Crossover | Buy & Hold |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total return | 142.92% | 338.94% |
| Annualized return | 7.92% | 13.54% |
| Annualized volatility | 11.35% | 17.59% |
| Sharpe ratio (rf=0) | 0.70 | 0.77 |
| Maximum drawdown | -29.24% | -33.72% |
| Buy signals | 26 | - |
| Sell signals | 27 | - |
| Time in market | 72.45% | 100.00% |

## Findings

- **The strategy did not beat buy-and-hold.** It returned 7.9% a year against 13.5%, with a lower Sharpe ratio (0.70 against 0.77).
- **It reduced overall risk.** Volatility fell from 17.6% to 11.4%. Most of the protection came in the 2020 crash, when the strategy moved to cash early and avoided most of buy-and-hold's 34% fall.
- **It was hurt by false signals in trendless markets.** In 2022–23 the price crossed the moving averages repeatedly, and the strategy sold after declines and bought back after rallies. Its drawdown in that period was deeper and longer than buy-and-hold's.
- **The result holds across window lengths.** None of the pairs tested (10/30, 20/50, 50/100, 50/200) beat buy-and-hold on return or Sharpe ratio. Shorter windows reduced drawdowns the most: the 10/30 pair limited the maximum drawdown to 18.1%.

## Limitations and next steps

- One asset and one period, with no prolonged bear market such as 2008. Next: test sector ETFs, futures, and longer histories.
- Cash earns 0%. Next: credit interest at the Treasury bill rate.
- Window lengths are fixed. Next: walk-forward testing, choosing windows on one period and evaluating them on the next.

## How to run

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
jupyter notebook ma_crossover.ipynb
```

Then run all cells (Kernel > Restart & Run All). Change the ticker, dates, window lengths, or cost in the Parameters cell.
